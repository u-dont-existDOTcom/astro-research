# Ptolemy III.13 — separate Mercury/Moon candidates with conditional fifth-mode claims

23 November 2026. A source-conditional sensitivity study, not an authorially unique soul governor or a tested human personality model.

## Primary-source decisions

I re-read Robbins's 1940 public-domain translation of [III.2–3](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Ptolemy/Tetrabiblos/3A%2A.html), [III.13](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Ptolemy/Tetrabiblos/3D%2A.html), and [I.7, I.13, I.23–24](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Ptolemy/Tetrabiblos/1B%2A.html). These are the digitized Loeb witness; the prior [printed-source collation](ptolemy_i24_wolf_1559_original_commentary_orb_adjudication_20261122.md) preserves the actual Wolf 1559 commentary and its uncertainty about the numerical distance. No new Greek manuscript collation is claimed.

* III.2, Loeb p.233: the five ways of domination are triplicity, domicile, exaltation, term, and phase or aspect. III.3 p.239 directs attention to the number of claims, then separately to power, location and mixture. We do not import the birth-time rectification tie-break from III.2 into this topic.
* III.13 pp.333–339: Mercury concerns reasoning and the Moon the sensory side, including lunar applications and separations. The common governor of both places is a particularly effective possibility under suitable conditions, not an exclusive requirement for every interpretation. Preserve separate Mercury and Moon candidates, their mixtures, and unresolved alternatives.
* I.13 pp.73–75 enumerates the traditional sign aspects and their angles. I.24 p.115 requires a small interval for application/separation, and ecliptic-side latitude agreement for bodily contacts. The older original 1559 commentary describes speed-dependent distance; neither 3° nor 15° is asserted to be Ptolemy's universally binding orb.
* I.7 p.43 assigns Sun/Jupiter/Saturn to day, Moon/Venus/Mars to night and Mercury according to its phase. III.13 qualifies effectiveness with familiar houses or sects and contrary testimony. I.23 discusses familiarity by multiple relations; I.24 and III.3 also consider solar motion and position relative to the horizon. Therefore **own domicile, fixed sect and a possible opposing-sect square are separate partial witnesses, not exhaustive power criteria**.
* Robbins's III.13 note 137 points to the anonymous commentary cited in III.4 note 22 on overcoming by zodiacal precedence. This study treats a preceding square by a body of the opposite fixed sect as one possible sign-based witness. It does not claim that such an aspect must overcome a particular planet, or that it establishes any personality effect.

There is no need to pretend a unique deterministic interpretation exists before developing a falsifiable **explicitly conditional** model. Original III.1 leaves the combination of many elements to the practitioner. A fixed, transparent reconstruction can be compared against alternatives; a result conditional on that reconstruction cannot be presented as the only historical doctrine.

## Actual experiment

The [engine](../../tools/astrohd/scripts/ptolemy_iii13_five_mode_aspect_century_v01.py) reuses the already audited four-mode votes independently at Mercury and the Moon, across all 3,506,400 original quarter-hour UTC timestamps at one fixed research site. Two printed term witnesses × two triplicity co-ruler policies form the four existing variants. For each variant, cross:

- 3° or 15° closeness sensitivity, not an authored historical threshold;
- Mercury traditional sign rays or degree-near rays; Moon requires degree-near rays as an application/separation proximity proxy;
- fifth-aspect eligibility for the earlier four external planets only, or all distinct classical bodies (a planet never aspects itself in this geometric calculation).

This yields 32 declared combinations. The previous four-mode claims are not removed from any planet by the fifth-aspect inclusion scope.

One geometric nonbodily claim contributes at most one fifth-mode vote per planet and place. A near bodily conjunction is treated as possible but not known, with no invented latitude. Exact lunar rays are geometrically admitted without assigning an applying/separating direction. The sign of the planet's longitude relative to the nearest ray is a zodiacal-precedence witness, not a computed exact event or a speed-based proof.

Two bounds are used. In the aspect-only sensitivity, the lower score is the four-mode count plus nonbodily ray membership; the upper score can additionally include a possible bodily claim. In the broader unresolved-phase sensitivity, each planet may have an unknown fifth-mode phase claim, capped at one fifth vote in total. The latter is a conservative mathematical envelope, not an assertion that all such phase assignments are physically or historically realizable. A body is uniquely selected only if its lower bound is strictly larger than every competitor's upper bound. Possible top sets retain any planet whose upper score reaches the maximum lower score. Near-horizon day/night sect is unresolved rather than imputed.

Every one of the 108 original planet/site/HD-index binary shards is checked against its SHA. The original 288,908 HD structural intervals and 125 near-boundary unresolved timestamps are kept; the previous 4,553 all-four common leaders robust to one unknown fifth claim must be conserved.

## Results

| Selection stable across all corresponding source variants | UTC samples | Same planet | Different planets | Distinct HD intervals |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Four modes only: same ordered pair under all four original terms/triplicity choices | **601,375** | Not split in this baseline | Not split in this baseline | Not counted |
| All 32 fifth-aspect variants, bodily claim uncertain | **139,741** | 34,470 | 105,271 | 16,175 |
| All 32, also allow an unknown phase claim | **29,676** | 8,467 | 21,209 | 3,678 |

The earlier **4,553** common-body cases with a four-mode lead of at least two votes are retained in both wider analyses. They are not the entire set of potentially meaningful Mercury/Moon candidates. A same-body vote maximum is not by itself the special dual configuration described in III.13.

### One declared historical branch, varying the closeness sensitivity

| Ptolemy printed terms, principal triplicity, degree-near rays, all distinct bodies | Both places uniquely selected | Same body | Different bodies | Neither or one place unresolved |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 3° | 1,558,138 | 298,478 | 1,259,660 | 1,948,262 |
| 15° | 940,412 | 179,163 | 761,249 | 2,565,988 |

A broader orb need not increase unique selections: additional possible claims can introduce ties. No source branch selects a different strictly unique winner at a place where the four-mode winner was already strictly unique merely by adding one claim per body: a one-vote addition can tie, but cannot overtake a one-vote lead. Newly selected cases arise from formerly unresolved four-mode cases.

The preceding-square interpretation is illustrative and does not adjudicate the actual force of contrary testimony. The 5,049 near-horizon sect-uncertain samples are retained as unknown rather than imputed. None of the counts are human observations.

## What the result does and does not support

The previous study's common-ruler screen is a narrow subset. The two-place comparison is the more relevant basis for a conditional model of the two kinds of source testimony. Different provisional bodies do not mean the chart fails the historical theory. The unresolved phase/latitude and sect witness flags are explicitly preserved, rather than inserted as unverified positive/negative personality points.

The [anonymous aggregate](../../tools/astrohd/reference/research/ptolemy_iii13_five_mode_aspect_century_public_v01.json) records every tested method and the cross-method intersections. It is source-only, not a human sample. The original source files and prior receipts were not overwritten.

**Next scientific step:** use the existing behavioral-pattern and source-contrast protocols to freeze a *bounded*, pre-exposure source-to-observable mapping. An unresolved source detail may be excluded or modeled as an explicitly labeled alternative, not automatically made a mandatory blocker of every exploratory model. A unique worldwide natal rank would require a declared candidate universe and a complete score search or a proven safe pruning bound. A full exhaustive finite-grid evaluation does not need a pruning bound; it must honestly state its finite time/location scope. Independent consented held-out human observations and null controls are needed for predictive validation, not for reporting a transparent conditional exploratory ranking. No empirical effect size or human rank was generated here.

All individual natal data, interviews, prior individual scores and original source scans remain in their authorized private archive.
