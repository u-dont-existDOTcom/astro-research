## 1. Ptolemy: what the passages permit

- *Tetrabiblos* **III.2** specifies five kinds of planetary familiarity: triplicity, domicile, exaltation, term and **phase or aspect**. Its closer-to-Ascendant-degree tie-breaker belongs to **birth-time rectification**; it is not an automatically portable III.13 temperament rule. [Primary text in Robbins's Loeb translation](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Ptolemy/Tetrabiblos/3A%2A.html).
- **III.3** instructs the reader to assign rulership to planets with more claims where several compete, and then evaluate their nature, strength, sect, positions relative to the angles, and combinations or contrary testimonies. The passage does **not** give a universally deterministic tie-breaker, integer weighting of every testimony, or a fixed numerical fifth-mode orb. [III.2–3](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Ptolemy/Tetrabiblos/3A%2A.html).
- **III.13** examines Mercury for reason and Moon plus its applications and separations for the sensory part. It gives **special importance**, not an exclusive necessary condition, to a governor relating to Mercury by aspect **and** the Moon by application/separation. Strength and contrary disposition remain relevant. [III.13](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Ptolemy/Tetrabiblos/3D%2A.html).
- **I.24** characterizes lunar application/separation using zodiacal precedence and following at sufficiently small distances; bodily conjunction also needs compatible ecliptic latitude. Our frozen grid lacks planetary latitude. The editor's report of **15°** from an anonymous commentator (Wolf, p.51) is **not Ptolemy's own numeric orb**. Sampling relative longitudinal motion is not automatically the same thing as the original textual criterion. [I.23–24, editor notes 127–131](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Ptolemy/Tetrabiblos/1B%2A.html).

The source-qualified numerical exercise takes each of Mercury and Moon **separately** and counts the four already-specified historical modes for seven traditional planets. This merely proves that a hypothetical **single extra vote per competing planet** could not tie that four-mode lead; it does not prove Ptolemy intended this full vote arithmetic or that strength could not change the reading.

## 2. Lilly CVII: corrected source scope

The modern type reproduction of Lilly, *Christian Astrology* III, chapter CVII, printed **pp.535–536**, was visually checked page by page. It says the first significator condition is a planet occupying the **“Sign ascending, or which is intercepted”**. The same chapter later speaks of planets **“in the Horoscope”** and **“in the ascendant”**. These expressions cannot be reduced, without argument, to *only* bodily occupancy of the quadrant first house. [Checked reproduction, pp.535–536](https://cdn.astmd.gr/attachments/2023/11/29/williamlilly500-543.pdf).

A [1647 original printing](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/s7z7c46y) is identified, but its **actual pp.535–536 images have not yet been directly collated**.
