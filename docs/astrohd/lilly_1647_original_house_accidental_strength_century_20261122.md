## One original fortitude rule now actually computable

Lilly's original **1647 first printing**, *Christian Astrology*, printed **p.115** (PDF page **149**), contains a numerical fortitude/debility table. This pass isolates **only its house-placement component** for each of the seven traditional planets and explicitly abstains from calculating a complete planetary-strength measure. Original scan PDF SHA-256: `2cb53e20e122ffc6e47917ba10241f6c49687cd7a4696f3b5db655a2a1aab28b`; source p115 rendered image SHA-256: `fe75468d8eaf129a1f643d6d652455e81eb95b0aedd3016091e3ce7c204e4aab`. The original page image is archived privately; [original Wellcome catalogue](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/s7z7c46y).

| Original first-print source house | Accidental house-only points |
|---|---:|
| 1st (Ascendant) and 10th (Midheaven) | +5 |
| 4th, 7th, and 11th | +4 |
| 2nd and 5th | +3 |
| 9th | +2 |
| 3rd | +1 |
| 6th and 8th | −2 |
| 12th | −5 |

The score is **not** the planet's full fortitude. Reception, detriment, fall, peregrine conditions, direct/retrograde and slow/fast motion, waxing/waning condition, planetary aspects, solar beams, historic nodal positions, and original fixed-star positions all remain independently relevant source conditions. We did **not** combine the house points with the earlier five-positive-component score to pronounce any complete manners ruler.

Lilly's **original p.115** table scores *planetary house placement*. We preserve two historically distinct astronomical house divisions: **Regiomontanus** (a traditional candidate for Lilly's practice) and **Placidus** (the independently preserved original research-grid site cache). Neither is silently promoted to a uniquely mandatory implementation. The program assigns each of seven classical planets to **one and only one of 12 oriented cusp-to-cusp house arcs** at each UTC sample, using inclusive beginning and exclusive ending cusps.
