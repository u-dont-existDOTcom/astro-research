## Same-row comparison semantics

For each row and model family, record the exact lowest and highest score *difference from the reference under that same variant*. Do not compare one candidate's maximum score to the reference's minimum from a different model. Preserve whether at least one variant ties exactly and whether all variants are computable. Use the frozen scorer's numerical tolerance.

For any fixed selection of traditions, report:

- Strictly higher in every included system, including HD.
- HD tied while strictly higher in every other included system.
- Weak dominance (at least as high in every included system) and Pareto improvement (weak dominance with at least one strict improvement).
- Equal scores in every system, with explicit existential and universal variant interpretations.
- Strictly higher in each separate HD-plus-other-family average, which is not the same as strict individual-system dominance.

For every criterion distinguish **any variant combination** (existence of one choice in each family) from **every variant combination** (the same row satisfies the criterion for all declared choices). The former is permissive and can exploit different choices for different candidates; it is not a single fixed predictive model. The latter is more stringent but still only conditional point-score robustness. Use a common usable-row mask across the included models and do not turn calendar or HD boundary exclusions into lower scores.

Report Ptolemy and Lilly separately; also treat either as a single Western alternative class, and report the stronger requirement of outperforming both. No arbitrary extra Western vote is introduced. In a distinct exploratory calculation, give HD, one Western model, Vedic and Chinese equal weight (one quarter each). Extrema of the sum over the Cartesian model product are exact under the independently declared choices; report how many rows score higher under any and under every choice. This additional arithmetic sensitivity is not a fitted or validated weighting rule. Do not infer exact existence of a composite tie merely from an interval crossing zero.

A saturated HD rubric can give zero strictly higher HD scores by construction. This cannot establish uniqueness. Report HD ties, other-system improvement and unresolved rows separately. Counts are timestamps at a single research location, not unique people or all possible birth locations and instants.

Store exact grid indices of core survivor sets only in the immutable private output, not in public Git; they designate hypothetical clock samples, not identified human subjects. ## Executed outcome and stricter evidence-coding comparison

The complete full-grid run processed all 3,506,400 original timestamps and replayed exactly all 1,117 included standalone and HD-plus-family marginal rank pairs per evidence coding. The shared usable grid contains 3,505,900 rows; unresolved source and calendar rows remain outside the intersection, not lower-scoring rows. Both original HD rank denominators reproduced. Exact reference scores, original source receipts, binary inputs and private output digests were checked.

The additional [cross-coding runner](../../tools/astrohd/scripts/compare_multitradition_cross_coding_v01.py) checked the entire original grid for the necessary HD-no-lower condition under both observation codings, then evaluated every eligible row with both codings using identical interpretation tags. The frozen reference attains the HD rubric ceiling; this exact logical reduction is sufficient for separate-system dominance, but cannot be used as a shortlist for averaged-score rank. The paired sign of each matched arm is evaluated before family extrema. In particular a model that succeeds only under one coding cannot be combined with a different model succeeding only under the other and called a shared interpretation. The stricter runner stores exact private survivor indices, and verifies that all strict survivors belong to the intersection of the corresponding prior independently evaluated coding sets. For universal strict survivors it requires exact equality to that intersection. Its three additional synthetic tests verify alignment and reject switched-variant successes. It takes the same private inputs, plus --overlap pointing to the first run's immutable receipt and a separate new --output.

Both executions completed and their digests and restrictive private permissions were verified. The current HD score's upper ceiling makes literal strictly-higher-in-HD-and-every-other-system impossible, but permissive same-row competing timestamps with equal HD and higher scores in all other systems exist, including under the same interpretation choices in both codings. Requiring weak superiority across every included composite variant in both codings leaves only a small local cluster around the exact reference clock, not a proof of historical or empirical uniqueness. No timestamp strictly improves every other composite family under every included interpretation in both codings. Equal-family averaged comparisons have different outcomes and are explicitly separate. All individual counts, date hypotheses and reference times remain private.

The next decision-relevant step is to inspect the exact survivors using unchanged rules, compare their trait-level evidence and null controls, and expand genuinely discriminating non-bespoke observables rather than repeatedly optimizing an exposed reference. Independent held-out birth records and observed behavior remain necessary for predictive validation. Do not turn a ceiling score or a stringent all-variants intersection into a probability, a global uniqueness claim, or a claim about actual people born at hypothetical times.
