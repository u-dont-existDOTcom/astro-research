## Why this is not another all-seven-body score

The prior [integrated partial natal-body strength census](lilly_1647_integrated_partial_fortitude_full_century_20261123.md) ranked a subset of original dignity, house-position and motion points for all seven traditional planets. But the **original first stage of William Lilly's chapter CVII** does not simply say to choose whichever planet has the biggest incomplete strength score anywhere in the chart.

I independently re-examined the actual **1647 first printing**, chapter CVII **printed p.535** (one-based scanned PDF 569). The original PDF is archived privately with SHA-256 **2cb53e20e122ffc6e47917ba10241f6c49687cd7a4696f3b5db655a2a1aab28b**, and the directly viewed original p535 page image has SHA-256 **49efe26a167fcdd6d0f1f08d3c7a69faaf87939b41cab02341ef6dc2500f4708**. [Original Wellcome edition](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/s7z7c46y).

The page starts with a first-stage occupant of the **“Sign ascending, or which is intercepted”** as a principal significator. It then says that **if several planets occupy the horoscope, all signify manners**; the more powerful has a more durable effect. It also allows **other planets with dignity in the occupant's place to participate**, and cautions that luminaries matter chiefly in a general way unless very strongly fortified.

These direct original words imply that the primary-occupant set must be computed **before** strength can decide between eligible planets. Other dignity-based participation, full historic strength, the original effect of luminaries and final human behavior remain unresolved.

## Three independently declared original-place interpretations

Because the first sentence is sign-based while subsequent wording says *horoscope*, the script retains three rival original-source spatial interpretations, not one retrospectively chosen method.

| Source interpretation | What counts as an eligible first-stage occupant? |
|---|---|
| **Ascending 30° zodiac sign** | All seven classical planets within the rising sign, regardless of which side of the quadrant-house cusp they fall |
| **Actual Regiomontanus first house** | Classical planet between the first and second quadrant cusps, with exact cusp-inclusive/cusp-exclusive geometry |
| **Ascending or fully intercepted first-house sign** | Planet in rising sign or in a complete 30° sign enclosed between first-house cusps |

For **no occupant**, the record says **later-stage fallback required**, never “negative personality evidence.” With **one**, record a **sole provisional primary occupant**, noting the original luminary/other-dignity caveats. With **several**, retain all as participating primary planets. Only their **partial** strength is compared, with uncertain motion scored as the interval −5 to +4, and strict preference allowed only if one body’s lower score exceeds every other *eligible* body's upper score, with sect known.

This is not a full historical Lilly winner. The method does not treat a planet that is ineligible by first-stage placement as principal merely because its incomplete body strength is high.
