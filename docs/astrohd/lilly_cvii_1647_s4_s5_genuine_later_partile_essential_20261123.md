1. **Which reference bodies must a single planet aspect?** A source-sensitive method treats a partile to **either** Moon or Mercury as enough to be a *possible* fourth-stage candidate; a stricter alternative requires the **same planet** to partile-aspect **both**. Sun, Venus, Mars, Jupiter and Saturn are the five currently operationalized external candidate bodies. Moon or Mercury are not regarded as self-aspecting themselves.
2. **“Forcibly” is not defined by the partile geometry alone.** We can count planets geometrically showing that numbered-degree relationship, but **cannot assert they qualify under Lilly's required full planetary strength**, which remains historically incomplete. Thus the output is **S4_PARTILE_POSSIBLE**, never a source-certified final ruler. 3. **How to combine essential dignity rights at Mercury and Moon's positions?** Original p.101–105/p.104's positive domicile/exaltation/triplicity/terms/face **5/4/3/2/1** values and original terms table differ from two printed Ptolemaic/Egyptian term witnesses; both **sum of rights at two places** and **maximum rights at one place** are modern explicitly declared interpretations. They must **not** be silently chosen to favor a participant's chart. This is a *rights at Moon/Mercury PLACES* question, **not** the strength of each natal planet at its own zodiacal position.
