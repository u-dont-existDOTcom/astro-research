### Original first printing, historical support and limitations

The directly inspected **1647 first edition** of William Lilly, *Christian Astrology*, printed **pp.535–536**, supplies the stage order: first-stage occupants of the ascending or intercepted sign, then (if none) planets joined to Moon or Mercury, then the Ascendant lord under a dispositor-beholding requirement, then a *forcible* partile to Moon/Mercury, and finally essential-dignity rights at the **places of Mercury and Luna**. The source also says that **several first-stage occupying planets all participate** in manners and that secondary nonoccupant planets having dignity in a primary's place can participate.

## Fully explicit one-clock ordered model

The new [source-reproducible engine](../../tools/astrohd/scripts/lilly_cvii_ordered_stage_coverage_full_century_v01.py) applies the *same chronological* source possibilities to all 3,506,400 original 15-minute UTC timestamps of **1926-11-19 inclusive to 2026-11-19 exclusive**, at **one original fixed research location**, not all continuous worldwide birth times. It source-SHA-checks 36 original planetary, 36 original site/angle and 36 HD-index binary shards individually, and independently audits **288,908 HD structural intervals** and the **125 near-transition unknown HD clock states**. Original conditional Ptolemy four-variant 4,553 common Mercury/Moon possible lords remain a separate **structural** reference, not a historically completed Ptolemaic soul governor or human validation.

All previous source conditions are retained as **96 independent stage-path policies**: three first-stage spatial modes, two printed 1647 p107 moiety envelopes, two S2 candidate-body scopes, two ASC-lord dispositing aspect interpretations, two other-planet-aspector interpretations, and two p106/p536 partile interpretations. The S5 candidate is tested under the previously published **six alternative** three-printed-terms × two researcher sum/max Mercury+Moon place-rights conventions.

Each source interpretation classifies every original UTC clock sample into exactly **one** of eleven clearly distinguished statuses:

Only the first, third, fifth and tenth categories contain a *provisional single-primary body*. For an identical UTC sample, an all-96 conditional candidate is counted only if all alternative historical stage interpretations give **the same stage category AND the same planet**. This is intentionally more conservative than adding unrelated stage candidate counts or selecting the highest-scoring natal planet globally.

In particular **S4 possible cases are NEVER counted as completed source-selected rulers**, even when the geometric contact involves a single planet. A unique p536 original fifth-stage positive-only score remains explicitly provisional. Full original strength, dignity-bearing secondary S1 participants, Ptolemy's fifth mode, and human predictive validity are **not** established by this algorithm.
