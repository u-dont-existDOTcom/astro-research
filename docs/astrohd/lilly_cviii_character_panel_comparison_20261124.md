## Distinct textual source

The [Lilly operational map](../../tools/astrohd/reference/research/lilly_cviii_planet_character_operational_v01.json)
uses CVIII, printed pp539–543, of the publicly accessible
[World Astrology transcription](https://libraryofagartha.com/Occultism/%27Aryan%27%20Occultism/Astrology/astrology%20books/William-Lilly-Christian-Astrology-1647-volume-3.pdf).
The accessible text labels a second edition; PDF image access was refused
by the host. This is a text-witness reading, not a new first-edition
facsimile collation. The earlier CVII first-print source studies remain
the authority for the implemented historical stage alternatives.

CVIII gives distinct descriptions for five classical planets under
strong/fortunate and weak/unfortunate conditions. It discusses the
luminaries as modifiers and gives examples of planetary mixtures rather
than a complete mechanically defined set of pair combinations. It does
not justify assigning separate complete standalone CVIII character lists
to the Sun and Moon.

Reuse the frozen Ptolemy experiment's observable vocabulary and evidence
denominators, but derive directions independently from Lilly's own text:
five planet rows, two source condition clauses each, 44 selected signed
directions. The map intentionally omits descriptors that cannot be
cleanly assessed from the shared behavioral definitions, including
criminality, health, sexual and identity claims. Source-unmentioned
traits are not negative predictions. Strong/weak is not good/bad moral
character.

## Scoring and historical scope

The [private panel runner](../../tools/astrohd/scripts/compare_lilly_character_panel_v01.py)
reuses all 96 previously implemented CVII ordered-stage variants, the
six-way positive-place S5 candidate consensus and the nine S1
secondary-participant possibilities. It recomputes exact Regiomontanus
house cusps for the existing chart inventory and checks the recorded
ascendant. No new chart selection or private trait recoding is introduced.

Keep all primary occupants and joined candidates in S1/S2, and the
previous source-conditional single candidate in S3/S5. Do not fabricate
a single ruler for multiple occupants, unresolved SX/SY, unadjudicated
S4 force or unresolved S5. Preserve S1 secondary participants certain
under every term interpretation and possible under any of the term
interpretations. The original secondary source only covers S1.

Four fixed exploratory arms: strict single-primary score or equal
mean across all primary participants, crossed with all-strong and
all-weak clauses. A luminary has zero character-list credit rather than
a substituted body. The strict single arm abstains on plural primaries;
the mean arm is a researcher-defined mixture, not Lilly's historical
relative strength calculation. Reuse the two-domain fixed denominators,
the equal HD/Western family weighting and the two unchanged evidence
codings from the previous planet-character comparison.

For each source path retain a deliberately loose interval allowing
any convex combination of the character contributions of primary
and possible S1 secondary participants, both clauses and abstention.
Where no primary is resolved, allow all bodies in the outer relaxation;
this is not a claim of actual eligibility. This bounds only the
selected character vocabulary and hypothetical mixture, not complete
Lilly doctrine. Luminary modification, full fortitude and mixed
character clauses remain unresolved. No weight or condition is chosen
from the reference's rank.

## Executed pilot

The private runner scored all 15 chart units from the previous
HD-selected comparison, all 96 stage paths and four fixed arms plus
the interval, under both frozen observation codings. The different
observation versions produce materially different rankings; no stable
unique first-place conclusion follows. The results do not justify a
full-grid or independently validated rank. The century extension was
not executed in this pass. Retain and verify the private receipts;
do not publish any individual primary sets, scores, rank counts,
birth inputs or interview evidence.

Next: extend the exact same fixed primary-character experiment to
the entire original grid, reconciling all earlier 96 stage-source
counts and preserving the same denominators and unknowns. Any
historically conditioned strong/weak qualification is a separate
source-sensitive model. Compare with existing Ptolemy alternatives
without treating the two historical traditions as independent evidence.
