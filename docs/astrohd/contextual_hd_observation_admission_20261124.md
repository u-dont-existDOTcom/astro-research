## Generic instrument repair specification

The following revisions are proposed source-neutral measurement improvements, not new scored astrology rules. They do not assume that the phenomena are unusual or that the chart's assigned categories predict them. Keep the original questions and their old score results intact. A revised instrument must use new versioned identifiers and a new predeclared, source-qualified observable mapping before scoring.

| Construct | Defect in a binary or underspecified item | Better behavioral measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Identity and direction | Treating new external information as loss of inner direction; conflating values, goals, plans and outcomes. | Separate persistence of core values and sense of identity from choosing a different course in changed circumstances. Ask about comparable contexts with unchanged stakes and resources, and how long a change persists after leaving. |
| Initiation | Every action has antecedents and enabling conditions; availability of an opportunity is not the source of the goal. | Record when a person first wanted a goal, what made it feasible, what prompted action, and whether similar actions occur without an explicit request. |
| Recognition | Accurate corrective feedback can improve a skill independently of being recognized; praise can improve mood without performance. | Compare willingness and performance under similar conditions and equivalent useful feedback, distinguishing accurate acknowledgment, generic praise and instructional correction. |
| Collaboration entry | Confounds invitation, consent, resources, competence, autonomy, and selection of partners. | Separate consensual self-initiation, invitation, and imposed entry. Record successful and unsuccessful cases with similar roles and resources, motivation, and observed outcomes. |
| Work endurance | Indefinite work and disproportionate recovery are not exhaustive opposites. | Compare defined normal, sustained, and overload workloads. Record hours, physical versus cognitive demand, sleep, interest, and subsequent recovery separately. |
| Emotional resonance | Feeling for another, sharing their emotion, amplification, personal stakes, and intentional emotional regulation are different. | Use separate intimate, familiar, and unfamiliar interpersonal contexts. Record automatic felt change, intensity relative to baseline, change after separation, and deliberate regulation. Do not infer a global trait from one relationship context. |
| Self-worth | Purposeful effort, approval seeking, competition, and overcommitment need not coexist. | Separate intrinsic goals from contingent self-worth and actual promises beyond capacity; record recurrence and consequences. |
| Task pressure | Prompt task handling may be rational prioritization, deadline response or habit rather than pressure relief. | Compare equally low-priority tasks with and without a deadline; distinguish relief when canceled from relief when completed and the experience of pending work. |
| Energy renewal | Renewal during engaging work and depletion after overload can both be true. | Record energy before, during and after comparable work at specified intensity and duration; distinguish ordinary fluctuations, rest, and prolonged overload. |
| Environmental influence | Companions and opportunities change everyone's feasible actions; comfort, belonging and core identity are not interchangeable. | Separate changed resources or circumstances from persistent changes in values, self-concept or priorities across comparable environments. |

Permit contextual and mixed descriptions, explicit absence, uncertainty, and examples. Ask neutral contrasts only when prior evidence cannot answer them; do not imply a preferred answer from known chart factors. A proposed trait's empirical discrimination requires comparing its distribution and measurement reliability across independently assessed people, not merely noting that alternative chart predicates differ.
