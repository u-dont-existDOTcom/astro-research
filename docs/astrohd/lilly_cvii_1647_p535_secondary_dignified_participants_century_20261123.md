## Important correction to “sole first-stage significator”

The previous [original 1647 Lilly CVII full ordered S1–S5 procedure](lilly_cvii_1647_full_ordered_stage_coverage_20261123.md) distinguishes charts with **exactly one planet physically occupying** the first-stage Ascendant sign/house. That remains a correct count of **PRIMARY occupants**. However, those reports had not yet implemented an additional explicit clause **on the same first-edition page** about planets that **participate because they have dignity at the primary's place**.

I returned directly to the original first printing of William Lilly, *Christian Astrology*, Book III, chapter CVII, **printed page 535**, one-based PDF page 569. Original scanned book SHA-256 **2cb53e20e122ffc6e47917ba10241f6c49687cd7a4696f3b5db655a2a1aab28b**, original full-page rendered-image SHA-256 **49efe26a167fcdd6d0f1f08d3c7a69faaf87939b41cab02341ef6dc2500f4708**. The facsimile was read visually, not inferred from a modern retyped paraphrase. [First-print catalogue](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/s7z7c46y).

Lilly begins with a principal occupant of the rising or intercepted sign. He then states that another planet also participates if it **“hath dignity in the place of the Significator of Manners”**. He later explicitly allows several planets occupying the horoscope to participate, while differentiating their relative strength and durability. His following paragraphs also qualify the effect through benevolent/malevolent nature, aspect, strength, disposition and, for Sun and Moon, exceptional fortification.

**Source-critical interpretation:** the body in the rising place remains **PRIMARY**. Another classical planet that has one or more positive essential dignities **at that primary's exact natal zodiacal longitude** becomes a **potential SECONDARY participant**, not another independently occupying first-stage body and not automatically a source-authorized full manners ruler. If there are multiple primary occupants, each one's zodiac position is examined and the **union of distinct non-primary dignity-holders** is formed. A body already primary is not counted again as a new secondary.

This particular source-bound implementation uses Lilly's original first-print **page 104** positive essential dignities at a **place**: domicile (+5), exaltation (+4), sect triplicity (+3), term (+2), and face (+1). A body has a potential positive essential claim when **any one** applies; the size of that claim is **not** treated as a new proven behavioral effect or a final source-correct priority score. Three printed historical **terms** witnesses are retained as different calculation traditions: original Lilly 1647, Ptolemaic as printed by Robbins 1940, and Egyptian as printed by Robbins 1940. The later printed tables are **not** retrospectively attributed to Lilly's first printing.

The original p.535 term *dignity* might require further interpretation concerning other fortitudes and actual participation; those conditions are preserved as incomplete, not silently assumed away.
