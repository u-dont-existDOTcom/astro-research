## The error in the completion language

The user was right to question that apparent inconsistency. Earlier progress updates treated “complete” as meaning *one named research phase had its own declared inputs or source pages covered*. But the owner's project was never simply to transcribe a source book or count astronomical positions. The parent outcome is a **historically supported, reproducible Human Design/Western/Vedic/Chinese/Gene Keys chart-to-observable-behavior comparison** across the declared candidate universe, with transparent handling of incompatible rules, independent evidence, and honest uncertainty.

Later newly inspected original passages exposed previously missing numerical components. New findings were genuine, but calling earlier narrower results “complete” without their exact denominator, method and historical boundary created a false impression. The owner should not have had to infer these distinctions across dozens of reports.
